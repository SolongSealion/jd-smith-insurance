This part is mainly for whoever looks after your IT. We have a more
detailed technical checklist we can give them.

5.1 What you need to know
    - Your web address, jdsmithinsurance.com (called the "domain
      name"), is paid up until December 2030.
    - The settings for that address are controlled today by your old
      web company, Interad / WSI. Their contact is
      badams@interad.ca.

      address is moved carelessly, office email can stop working.
      Nothing should be changed until all the email settings have
      been copied over first.

5.2 Steps
    1. Ask Interad / WSI, in writing:
       - Who is listed as the OWNER of jdsmithinsurance.com? It must
         be JD Smith & Associates Insurance Brokers Inc., not the web
         company. If it isn't, ask them to change it.
       - For a full copy of the address's technical settings (your IT
         person will know this as the "DNS zone file").
    2. Choose a new company to hold the address (for example your new
       website host, or a company such as Cloudflare, Namecheap or
       GoDaddy). Open the account in the office's name, using an
       email address that is NOT @jdsmithinsurance.com (for example a
       Gmail address). That way you can still get into the account
       if office email ever stops.
    3. Your IT person copies all the email settings to the new
       company first, and checks them with Microsoft 365 and
       Barracuda (your email providers).
    4. Ask Interad / WSI for the "transfer code" for the address.
    5. Start the transfer with the new company, using that code. It
       takes up to a week. Approve the confirmation email when it
       arrives.
    6. Test that office email still sends and receives.
    7. Then we switch the address over to the new website. We will
       keep the current website running for a few weeks, in case
       something was missed.
    8. We will also make sure old links (from Google, bookmarks and
       insurers' websites) lead to the right page on the new website.
