Meet the team

Management biographies

Dr. J. D. Smith, BA, CCIB, LLD (hon)

President

J. D. came to the insurance industry in 1979 with a background in Canadian publishing. He worked with his father for 16 years, publishing many Canadian best sellers and calendars of Canadian artists.

A graduate of Ryerson University, where he also taught business courses, he holds a BA in Economics and a BA in Sociology, and took the MBA program at York University. He received an honorary Doctor of Laws in 2006 for his community work.

He started the brokerage with one employee, the support of six insurance companies and one car policy. Within two years it insured car dealerships across Ontario, a hospital and eight large retail franchises. Today it is one of the 50 largest brokers in Ontario, with over 12,000 clients.

Our goal has stayed the same, too: Peace of Mind Insurance for every family and business we serve.

Call J. D. at ext. 234

Mark R. Smith
