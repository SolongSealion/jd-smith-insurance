We can help

Canada's relationship with the USA is changing. Tariffs have brought increasing problems to exporters and importers. You may find that all of a sudden you have to pay extra fees to receive your goods across the Kingston, Niagara Falls or Windsor borders.

We can help. We can usually issue you a customs bond in a couple of days.

How a customs bond works

  1. The bond is a contract between three parties: the importer (your company), the customs department (CRA) and the insurance company.
  2. With our standard bond of $50,000, the customs department knows that it will get paid, either by you or by the insurance company. Usually, they will release your shipment quickly.
  3. We will issue you a yearly, continuous bond that will help you get all your shipments through Canada customs quickly and will assist your customs broker at the border.

If you are looking for bonding in this tough business environment, please give us a call and complete our 2-page application.
