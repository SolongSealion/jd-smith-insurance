What to do after an accident

  1. Get the other driver's details. Their driver's licence number; the year, make, model and serial number of their vehicle (from the ownership); the licence plate number; and their insurance company and policy number (from the pink liability slip).
  2. Go to the Collision Reporting Centre. Take your vehicle to the Collision Reporting Centre in your area.
  3. The police complete a report. They will look at the damage to your vehicle and complete an accident report.
  4. Your insurance company gets a copy. The police send a copy of the report to your insurance company.
  5. Call your broker to report the accident. We will give your insurance company a full report to get your claim started.

Getting your car repaired

All insurance companies have "preferred shops" that they will refer you to for the repairs to your vehicle. These preferred shops guarantee their work to the insurance company.

Should you claim? Small claims and adjusters

We advise our clients not to put in small claims and lose the good claims discount they have with their company. Keep in mind that all claims are recorded and available to all companies for future policy rating. This helps keep the rates down for those of us with rare claims. Your broker can tell you what your deductible is and whether a claim is worth making.

The adjuster will help you with the claim and settle it with the insurance company. Be honest and clear with them. They are there to help, but also to protect you and me from fraudulent claims that increase our premiums.

Our companies are professional and prompt in settling legitimate claims. We work with them to prevent fraud. Fraud hurts us all. Please report suspected fraud.

Your accident benefits

If you are injured, your accident benefits help pay for treatment, care and lost income. Only the coverage in place at the time of the accident applies to your claim. All auto policies in Ontario changed in July 2026, and some benefits that used to be standard are now optional.
