How to make an online payment

Thank you for insuring with J D Smith & Associates, or J D Smith Insurance & Financial. If you do online banking with the institutions below, it is easy to pay your insurance policy issued by our office.

Pay through your bank Automatic monthly payments
Online banking bill pay

Pay in 5 steps

  1. Log in to your online banking.
  2. Select “add a bill”, “new bill” or “add payee”.
  3. When prompted to search for a payee, type in:
    J D SMITH & ASSOCIATES INS. BROKERS
    or
    J.D. SMITH ASSOCIATES INS. BROKERS
  4. You will be prompted to enter a 6-digit account number: the customer number at the top right of your invoice. If you do not have this number, contact your broker directly.
  5. Proceed to pay the bill.
Depending on which bank you use, there may be either spaces or periods between J and D. Make sure to include “& ASSOCIATES”.

What you need

  • Online banking with one of the banks below
  • Your 6-digit customer number, at the top right of your invoice
  • The amount due on your invoice
