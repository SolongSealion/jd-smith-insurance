Home and auto

12 ways to save

  1. 1

    Check your policy carefully for correct information

    Postal codes, ages and location addresses can affect your rate.

  2. 2

    Ask about the mature adult discount

    Are you getting the additional age discount for mature adults?

  3. 3

    Check your deductibles

    A higher deductible means a lower premium.

  4. 4

    Don’t pay for coverage you don’t need

    If your car is over 5 years old, you may want to take off the collision. Keep jewellery you don’t wear in a safety deposit box.

  5. 5

    Are you a non-smoker?

    Tell us. Many companies give a non-smoker discount on home insurance.

  6. 6

    Stay with one insurance company

    Have you been consistent with one company, or do you switch every few years? The companies give loyalty discounts.

  7. 7

    Install an alarm system

    In your home and in your car.

  8. 8

    Pay small claims yourself

    If you have a small claim, pay it yourself and save your insurance for the big one. Talk to your broker first.

  9. 9

    Combine your house and car policies

    For the extra discounts. Not all companies do this.

  10. 10

    Pay monthly, or look at a 3-pay policy

    Spread your premium out in a way that works for you.

  11. 11

    Pay your premium in full

    Some companies give a 5% discount if you pay your premium in full.

  12. 12

    Talk to one of our brokers

    A broker who has your interest in mind. Contact your broker.

Protect your vehicle

Car theft in Ontario and Toronto: what you can do

Ontario is seeing 130 cars stolen every day, and Toronto is the worst area. The police and OPP are trying, but are not having a lot of success. Your carmaker’s GPS will not help with a professional theft.

The main targets are high-end SUVs, Toyota Highlanders, Lexus, Mercedes and Range Rovers. See the top 10 most stolen cars in Ontario.

The best way: install a professional tracker hidden in the vehicle.

Technology is now the main way vehicles are stolen, using tools bought online. Here are popular theft methods and ways to deter them.

The diagnostic port

Method: once inside a vehicle, thieves plug into the onboard diagnostic port (OBD) and program a new key fob, then start the car and drive away. It is one of the biggest methods in Ontario, and it has grown six years in a row.

Defence: an OBD port lock that covers the port. Some owners fit a dummy port that does nothing. Mechanical steering-wheel or gas-pedal locks and wired-in immobilizers also help (immobilizers need professional installation so they don’t void your warranty).

The relay attack

Method: two thieves work together. One carries an antenna near your house to pick up your key fob’s signal while the other pulls the car’s door handle, and they use the signal to unlock and start the car.

Defence: keep your fob in a Faraday box or pouch that blocks its signal, at home or in your pocket or purse. In parking lots, don’t press the lock button again from a distance “just to make sure”: someone nearby can capture the signal.

Tracking tags

Thieves hide AirTags or SmartTags on cars they spot in a mall parking lot, follow them home, and come back at two or three in the morning. Check your car for tags you didn’t put there.

Other ways to deter theft or recover a stolen vehicle

  • GPS trackers installed by dealers or after-market shops, which send a stolen vehicle’s location so you can tell the police.
  • Park in well-lit areas, use a garage if you can, and always turn on the car’s security system.
  • Report suspicious activity, like people walking around your neighbours’ driveways at night. Organized groups scout neighbourhoods for vehicles without these precautions.
