What's covered

This endorsement allows you to carry passengers. It will help protect you if you are sued after an accident while you are driving for a ride-sharing service. We insure your vehicle and add this unique endorsement to your auto policy.

Third-party liability limits
$1 to $5 million
Ride-sharing time covered
Up to 20 hours a week

Who is eligible

Most good Uber drivers will be eligible for coverage.

  • You must be licensed for a minimum of six years in Canada or the USA.
  • Your vehicle can carry a maximum of eight passengers.
  • It is used for ride-sharing for up to 20 hours per week only.
  • No other commercial use of the vehicle is permitted.
  • The vehicle cannot be registered as a taxi, limousine or any other public transportation.
