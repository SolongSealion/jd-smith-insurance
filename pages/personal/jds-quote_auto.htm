Who is eligible

To get an online auto quote:

  • You should have a good driving record.
  • Have no more than 3 tickets in 3 years.
  • You must have a “G2” licence for at least 1 year.
  • Not more than 1 at-fault claim in the past 5 years.

Ontario auto reform 2026

All auto policies in Ontario have changed as of July 2026. Some accident benefits that used to be included in every policy, such as income replacement, caregiver and housekeeping benefits, are now optional.

We want you to stay informed and make wise choices for your coverage. Ask us before your renewal which options make sense for you.
