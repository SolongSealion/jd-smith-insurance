How we do business

Qualified professionals, working only for you

When you buy through an insurance broker, you are using the services of qualified professionals. JD Smith Insurance has over 30 licensed brokers, who understand your needs and will develop a solution for you. We operate independently of any insurance company… and solely on behalf of you, our customer.

When JD Smith Insurance acts on your behalf, a comprehensive range of coverage is fully accessible to you. You can be assured that we will do everything in our power to find the best protection to suit your specific requirements.

We know the importance of creating many business-to-business relationships with insurance companies who also stand for integrity and transparency, and who provide broad coverage with excellent claims service. We work closely with these insurance companies and our client base to choose the best selection of products available on the market to meet all of your needs.
