Life at JD Smith

What it's like to work here

We are a growing broker with a production team and a service team, working together to keep and grow our business. We insure a lot of churches, contractors and small businesses.

  • Your own clients. We assign each person specific personal lines accounts, so you get to know the clients you are servicing.
  • Great benefits. An excellent pension plan, a great work environment and a flex day system.
  • An easy commute. We're at Hwy 7 and Leslie, so there's no downtown traffic to contend with.
  • A modern office. We're 80% paperless, so you need to be good on computers.
  • The work. Billing, obtaining underwriting information and working with our many clients to give excellent personal service.
  • Who you are. You can work independently as well as part of a growing team of 30 brokers. You have a superior telephone manner and effective communication skills, and you like working with the public.
  • A team player is a must. We all work together to develop long-term clients, and we want someone looking for a long-term position to grow with our company.
