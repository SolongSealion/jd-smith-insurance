JDS builder's risk (course of construction) package

Our JDS Builders Risk (Course of Construction) Package offers project-specific property coverage and premises liability with limits up to $2,000,000 for new construction and renovations. It is designed for residential and light commercial buildings with wood frame construction. This policy can be purchased by the building owner, general contractor and construction manager.

Note: the policy period is available up to 24 months and allows backdating up to 14 days only.
