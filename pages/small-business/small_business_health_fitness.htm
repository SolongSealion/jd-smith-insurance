A package for licensed, certified or trained practitioners

JD Smith Insurance offers very competitive rates for customizable packages when purchasing E&O, CG liability and COED (contents of every description) coverages. Our JDS unique package is designed for health & wellness as well as fitness practitioners who are licensed, certified or trained to perform their primary modality.

This Canadian package is suitable for individuals who are working as independent contractors or business owners who rent/lease a location and have up to 20 employees.
